# WeNanny: finding the right nanny is a matching problem

Hiring care shouldn’t mean scrolling directories or guessing from WhatsApp threads. Families need structured comparison and a clear reason to trust a shortlist.

WeNanny is a live UAE marketplace that matches families with nannies on compatibility signals — not just availability.

[Visit WeNanny](https://www.wenanny.ae/)

---

## The challenge

Most care hiring still looks like this:

- Fragmented channels (WhatsApp groups, agencies, referrals) with no single source of truth
- Shallow profiles that make side-by-side comparison hard
- Decisions made on instinct because the product never explains *why* someone is a fit

The buyer problem isn’t “more listings.” It’s **confidence in the match** before investing time in conversations.

---

## What we built

### 1) Structured profiles built for comparison

- Family needs and nanny experience captured in a consistent format
- Skills, preferences, timing, and readiness surfaced the same way every time
- Less noise when building a shortlist

### 2) Compatibility-led matching

- Hard filters remove clear mismatches first
- Ranking surfaces suitability, not just “who is free”
- Fewer irrelevant introductions for both sides of the market

### 3) Explainable recommendations

- Each strong match comes with a short rationale families can understand
- Transparency beats volume when trust is the bottleneck
- Faster, more confident next steps

### 4) A hiring journey, not a brochure directory

- Product path from discovery toward onboarding and ongoing ops
- Built to learn from live usage and ship weekly
- Production codebase you can grow, not a static marketing site

---

## Outcome

WeNanny’s core loop is intentionally simple:

1. **Capture** needs and profiles with structure
2. **Match** with filters + compatibility ranking
3. **Explain** why a candidate fits
4. **Ship** improvements from real marketplace usage

The commercial takeaway for buyers: if your marketplace stalls on “search that feels random,” the fix is structured comparison and explainable fit — the same shape of problem we take through a Build & Launch sprint.
