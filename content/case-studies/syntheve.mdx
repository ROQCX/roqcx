# Syntheve: ATS Resume Checker & Builder

Syntheve helps job seekers understand why their resume is getting filtered out, then fix gaps immediately in a builder workflow.

[Visit Syntheve](https://www.syntheve.com/)

---

## The challenge

Most resume tools either:

- Tell you “use this template” (without showing what is actually wrong), or
- Require a long setup before you learn whether your resume is competitive for a specific role

We wanted a flow that gets to truth fast: **match score + diagnosis first**, then a **builder** that helps users close the gaps without starting over.

---

## What we built

### 1) ATS resume checker that explains the “why”

- Score against a real job description (not a generic checklist)
- Call out missing keywords and weak bullets
- Highlight formatting risks that get resumes misread by ATS

### 2) A builder workflow to fix issues immediately

- Turn diagnosis into edits (rewrite bullets, tighten structure, improve clarity)
- Make it easy to iterate: adjust, re-check, and improve

### 3) Performance + trust constraints

- **Fast time-to-value**: a useful output in ~30 seconds
- **Privacy posture**: resume processed in memory (not stored) as communicated on the public site

---

## Outcome

Syntheve’s core loop is intentionally simple:

1. **Check** (get a clear match score + diagnosis)
2. **Fix** (apply changes in the builder)
3. **Re-check** (ship a better resume with confidence)
