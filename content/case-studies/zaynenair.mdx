# zaynenair.com: the personal site, separate from the studio

Fractional product leadership lives on zaynenair.com. Studio delivery — websites, apps, automations and training — stays with ROQ CX. The personal site has to introduce the person and the work without becoming a second brochure.

[Visit zaynenair.com](https://www.zaynenair.com/)

---

## The challenge

The first screen has to say who the work is for, and it has to keep going as someone scrolls. A static poster would flatten a practice that moves from the problem, through the build, to what ships.

---

## What we built

The hero is one pinned screen. Scrolling moves it. The sequence is:

1. **Discover.** Name the problem that can pay, starting from the customer, the revenue and the constraint that decides the first version.
2. **Shape and build.** Strategy and build stay together. Product decisions and execution happen in one loop.
3. **Ship.** Launch, then scale what earns.

The stages are labelled Discover, Shape, Build and Ship. The picture is drawn on a canvas and follows the scroll, so the motion is part of the reading.

---

## Outcome

The site is live at zaynenair.com. It is the personal practice. ROQ CX remains the studio.
