# Emirates Dubai 7s: ticketing that holds at peak

Flagship sports weekends don’t forgive flaky entry. Fans need wallet-ready passes. Ops need scanning that stays up when gates open. Organizers need a post-event picture of who showed up — not a pile of PDF tickets.

For Emirates Dubai 7s, we shipped a digital ticketing platform and companion app under real calendar pressure: buy, transfer, and store tickets in-app; QR entry at the gate; schedules and news; payments; and a post-event insights pack for demographics and venue spend.

---

## The challenge

Before the rebuild, the event still ran on manual PDF ticketing. That left three buyer-visible gaps:

- **Fragile entry** — paper/PDF flows don’t scale cleanly at peak gate volume
- **Almost no usable attendee data** — limited demographics, spend, or engagement signal for the next season
- **Weak pre- and in-event engagement** — schedules, results, and updates lived outside the ticketing experience

The brief was clear: modernize entry, keep the weekend safe under load, and leave organizers with data they could actually use.

---

## What we shipped

### Digital ticketing & wallet

- Buy, transfer, and store passes in an in-app wallet
- Real-time QR validation at the gate
- Secure digital payments alongside ticket purchase

### Event experience

- Schedules, live results, and news in one place
- Team following and fixture tracking
- Digital merchandise paths with in-person collection

### Ops & insights

- Peak-hour scanning designed for concurrency, not demo traffic
- Post-event packs on demographics, engagement, and venue spend
- Cleaner fraud posture than printable PDFs

The architecture had one job above all others: **hold when thousands of fans hit the gates in the same hour.**

---

## Outcomes

The weekend ran on digital passes with measurable engagement and cleaner ops data for the next season:

- **80,000+** ticket passes distributed through the app
- **2M+** in-app interactions across the event
- **2,400+** digital payments processed
- **3,000+** tickets scanned per hour at peak

### What that meant for the buyer

- Faster, more reliable entry at the busiest moments
- Attendee and spend insight instead of guesswork for planning
- A platform shape that could grow (merchandise, team following) without redoing the core

> "The new ticketing platform transformed our event operations. It's user-friendly, efficient, and significantly boosted our ticket sales."  
> *Event Director, Emirates Dubai 7s*

**Commercial takeaway:** high-concurrency event products succeed when the sprint scope is ruthless — wallet, validation, and ops data first — then layer engagement once the gate flow is proven.
